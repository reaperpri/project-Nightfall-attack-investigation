# Student Challenges

The in-app challenge mode presents one question at a time and reveals feedback only after a response.

1. **Evidence or assumption?** Distinguish E-02's observed sign-in fields from a claim of account compromise.
2. **Find the ATT&CK mapping.** Identify a candidate for the process-query behaviour and explain why it remains an analyst assessment.
3. **Identify the missing evidence.** Name telemetry that would help establish what the outbound flow contained.
4. **Build the investigation map.** Add evidence-linked candidate techniques and rationale to the Navigator layer.
5. **Defend your conclusion.** State what is known, what remains suspected or unresolved, what is not established, and the next safe investigation step.

The challenge answers are teaching feedback, not incident verdicts.
